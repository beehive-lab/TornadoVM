Here is a two-sentence explanation of GPU acceleration:

GPUs (Graphics Processing Units) are specialized computer chips that can perform complex mathematical calculations much faster than traditional CPUs, making them ideal for tasks that require parallel processing, such as video rendering, scientific simulations, and data compression. By utilizing multiple processing units (cores) and memory, GPUs can accelerate certain types of computations, such as matrix operations and image processing, to speeds that are orders of magnitude faster than traditional CPUs.
